# Robustness and deployment realism (Roadmap v2, Phase 10)

Generated by `fraud-ae robustness`. Rolling-origin validation on the temporal split (models trained on the first 60 % of legitimate traffic; supervised and fine-tuned models also see the frauds of that window), seed / data-seed variance of the Phase 9 winners, and the evasion probe on the legit-only autoencoder.
